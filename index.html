Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 1, 2025  ·  Last updated: May 1, 2025

Who We Are

DM Engine is an AI-powered Dungeon Master app for tabletop roleplaying game sessions. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have over it.

What We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to run the app:

  • Account information — your email address (or Apple ID token) used to create and authenticate your account.
  • Campaign & session data — character sheets, campaign settings, session history, and DM responses you create inside the app. This is stored in our database so your campaigns persist across devices.
  • Usage data — anonymous metrics such as session count and feature usage to help us improve the app. No personally identifiable information is attached to these metrics.

We do not collect your real name, phone number, location, or payment card details.

AI-Generated Content

Your player inputs and campaign context are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate DM responses. Anthropic processes this data under their own privacy policy. We do not use your session content to train our own models. We recommend you do not enter real personal information into campaign narratives.

Voice & Audio

When voice narration is enabled, DM response text is sent to ElevenLabs to generate audio. This is processed transiently and not stored by us. ElevenLabs processes this data under their own privacy policy.

Image Generation

When scene image generation is enabled, scene descriptions are sent to fal.ai to generate images. Images are not linked to your account and are processed transiently. fal.ai processes this data under their own privacy policy.

How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate your account and keep your campaigns saved.
  • To generate AI Dungeon Master responses during your sessions.
  • To improve app stability and features using anonymous usage metrics.

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

Data Retention

Your campaign and session data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time by contacting us at the address below, and all associated data will be removed within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

DM Engine is rated 17+ and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data at any time. To make a request, contact us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

Security

We use industry-standard security practices including encrypted connections (TLS) and row-level security policies on our database. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will notify you via the app or email before the changes take effect. Continued use of the app after that point constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
